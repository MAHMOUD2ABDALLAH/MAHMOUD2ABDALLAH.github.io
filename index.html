About

Who I Am

A data analyst dedicated to turning complexity into clarity.

My Story

My journey into data analytics started with a simple frustration: too many organizations sit on valuable data but have no clear way to read it, trust it, or act on it.
Over the past 3+ years, I've dedicated my career to closing that gap.
I've collaborated across industries to design interactive dashboards, build automated reporting pipelines, and create self-service analytics tools that put insights directly into the hands of decision-makers.

What drives me is the business impact.
Seeing leadership make faster, smarter decisions because of something I built is what makes this work meaningful.
Beyond traditional analytics, I'm actively expanding into AI and machine learning, exploring predictive analytics and intelligent automation to take data analytics to the next level.

What I Do Best

  • Data Analytics and Dashboards: Designing executive-ready dashboards (Power BI, Tableau, Looker Studio) focused on clarity and storytelling.
  • Advanced Analytics and Python: Using Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn for statistical modeling and predictive solutions beyond standard reporting.
  • Process Automation: Building low-code automation with Microsoft Power Platform (Power Automate, Power Apps) to eliminate manual work.
  • AI and Machine Learning: Applying ML fundamentals, NLP, and deep learning (TensorFlow) to real business problems.

What Drives Me: Data without context is just noise.
My goal is to deliver clarity that helps people make better decisions, faster.
I bring curiosity to every dataset and a constant drive to keep learning.
Whether you need an analytics solution or want to explore what AI can do for your business, let's turn your data into something that works for you.
