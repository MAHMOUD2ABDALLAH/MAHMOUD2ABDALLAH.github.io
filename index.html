MY STORY

It began with a single question: could the signals from a patient’s body warn us before it is too late?
That question became my graduation project.
A ventilator monitor streaming live vital readings to an app, complete with a machine learning model to detect possible conditions.
The biggest lesson I learned had nothing to do with code: data only matters when someone can act on it.

That lesson followed me into freelancing.
Working with diverse clients on Upwork across e-commerce, retail, HR, and finance, I learned to translate messy data into clear, actionable answers.
I wasn't just writing scripts; I was solving real business problems and building a reputation for reliable, predictive insights.

Seeking to build more accessible solutions, I joined Microsoft’s Power Up Program.
There, I discovered the power of low-code automation and AI.
I built practical, user-friendly tools.
From library management systems to animal shelter databases, and learned that the best tools are the ones people use without a second thought.

I took that knowledge into the real estate sector, where I mentored junior analysts and designed BI dashboards that bridged the gap between sales and operations teams.
Around the same time, I stepped into the EdTech space, tackling my biggest challenge yet: consolidating massive, fragmented educational datasets from government ministries and Al-Azhar institutes.

Today, as a BI Analyst, I turn that immense complexity into one clear picture.
I build live dashboards that guide decisions for senior stakeholders and unify reporting across millions of users.

The mission remains unchanged: turning tangled numbers into decisions people can trust.
Behind every dataset stands a real person.
A patient.
A student.
A customer.
A team waiting for direction.
That is what keeps this work personal.
